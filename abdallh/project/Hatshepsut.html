Hatshepsut

  • Dynasty: 18th Dynasty
  • Period: New Kingdom
  • Date: c. 1478–1458 BC
  • Material: Limestone, Granite, Sandstone
  • Found in: Deir el-Bahari, Luxor
Description

The Mortuary Temple of Hatshepsut is a masterpiece of ancient architecture. Built into the sheer cliffs of Deir el-Bahari, it features three massive terraces connected by long ramps, reflecting her powerful and prosperous reign.

Did You Know?

Hatshepsut was one of the few female pharaohs and wore a false beard to assert her authority!
