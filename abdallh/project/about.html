Why This Exists

From the Nile to your screen

The Egyptian Museum project started as an attempt to make ancient Egypt feel close again — not behind glass in a distant hall, but searchable, walkable, and explained in plain language.

Every pharaoh, treasure, and dynasty here is presented the way a curator would explain it to a visitor standing in front of the case: what it is, when it's from, and why it mattered.
