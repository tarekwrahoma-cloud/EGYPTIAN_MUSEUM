Frequently Asked Questions

What are the museum opening hours?

The museum is open daily from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Ticket counters close at 4:00 PM.

How can I buy entry tickets?

Tickets can be purchased online through our website or directly at the museum entrance.

Are photography passes allowed?

Personal photography with phones is allowed. Professional cameras require a special permit at the ticket desk.
