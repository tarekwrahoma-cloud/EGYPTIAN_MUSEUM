Famous Mummies of Ancient Egypt

Preserved forever — the faces of a legendary civilization

Tutankhamun

The Boy King

Discovered in 1922 by Howard Carter. His nearly intact tomb became the most famous archaeological find in history. He ruled at a very young age and died around 18–19 years old.

Ramesses II

Ramesses the Great

One of the best-preserved royal mummies. He lived to about 90 years old and was one of Egypt’s most powerful and longest-reigning pharaohs.

Seti I

Father of Ramesses II

Considered one of the finest mummies ever found. His refined facial features and excellent preservation make him one of the most beautiful royal mummies.

Hatshepsut

The Female Pharaoh

One of the most successful female rulers in history. Her mummy was identified in 2007. She ruled as a full pharaoh and built magnificent monuments.

Thutmose III

The Napoleon of Egypt

A brilliant military leader who expanded Egypt’s empire to its greatest extent. His mummy shows a strong and determined face even after thousands of years.

Seqenenre Tao

The Brave King

His mummy clearly shows fatal battle wounds on the head. He most likely died fighting the Hyksos, making his remains one of the most dramatic royal mummies.
